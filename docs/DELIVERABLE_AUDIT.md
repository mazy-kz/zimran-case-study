## Audit conclusion

All explicit content requirements are covered. The PDF closes the original format gap. The main limitation is analytical rather than presentational: the one-week observational data cannot identify causal channel or landing-page effects, and Partner 2 revenue must be allocated across device cohorts because user-level revenue is unavailable. Both limitations are disclosed in the workbook and README.
